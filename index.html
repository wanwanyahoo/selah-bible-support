Daily Scripture, personal widgets, and complete chapters offline.

Contact support

Report an issue or ask for help through our support tracker. Include your device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue.

Open a support request

Add a widget

Save a named style in the app’s widget studio. Touch and hold your Home Screen, add a Selah widget, then edit it to choose your saved style. iOS controls the widget’s final size and appearance.

Languages and Bible editions

The interface language and Bible edition are separate choices in Settings. Each edition keeps its own wording, numbering, source, and attribution. Historical editions retain historical spelling. All bundled editions contain 66 books. Source and license details are available inside the app.

Photos and illustrations

Choose a built-in illustration or select your own photo through the system photo picker. Home cards, sharing images, and named widget styles can use different backgrounds.

Background music

Optional instrumental music is available in Settings. It starts only when enabled, with volume and sleep timer controls. The app does not provide synthesized Scripture narration.

Your reading stays private

No account, advertising SDK, or app analytics. Favorites, settings and selected photos are stored locally. Read the privacy policy.
