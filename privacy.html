Privacy Policy

Bible Verses & Widgets: Selah

Effective November 12, 2026.

Data collection

The app does not create an account, collect reading history on a server, use advertising SDKs, or include app analytics. We do not collect, sell, or share personal data from your use of the app.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, reading position, language and appearance settings, reminder preferences, widget styles, and photos you choose remain in the app’s local storage or its private shared container for widgets. The app does not upload these items to our servers. Your device’s operating-system backup settings may separately include app data.

Photos

The app uses the system photo picker. You choose the individual photos to provide; full photo-library access is not required. Selected photos are resized and re-encoded locally for display and sharing.

Reminders and audio

Reminders use local notifications after you grant permission. Optional bundled background music plays on the device. The app does not record microphone audio or provide speech synthesis.

Sharing and external links

When you choose to share text or an image, iOS opens your selected destination app. That destination’s policy applies. Bible source links open external websites. The app does not automatically upload your Scripture selections to those sites.

Support and this website

If you open a support request on GitHub, the information you choose to submit is handled through GitHub. This support site is hosted on GitHub Pages; hosting requests are processed by GitHub under its privacy policy. This website has no advertising or analytics scripts.

Removing local information

Remove favorites or selected photos inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data according to iOS behavior; device backups are managed separately in your Apple settings. No account deletion is needed because there are no app accounts.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support tracker.
